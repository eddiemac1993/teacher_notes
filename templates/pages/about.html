About Teacher Notes Zambia

A digital education platform designed to improve access to high-quality, curriculum-aligned learning materials while empowering teachers to earn from their professional knowledge.

Our Vision

We envision a Zambia where every learner has affordable access to verified, exam-ready learning resources, and where teachers are financially rewarded for sharing quality educational content.

Our Mission

To bridge the education resource gap in Zambia by creating a sustainable ecosystem that connects dedicated teachers with eager learners, ensuring quality education materials reach every corner of the country while recognizing and rewarding the hard work of our educators.

The Problem We Solve

For Students

  • • Difficulty finding reliable, syllabus-aligned notes that match ECZ standards
  • • Limited access to quality study materials, especially in rural areas
  • • Expensive textbooks and supplementary materials beyond many families' reach
  • • Uncertainty about content quality and exam relevance

For Teachers

  • • Quality teaching materials scattered across WhatsApp groups and USB drives
  • • Teachers share content freely but receive no compensation for their expertise
  • • No trusted system to verify engagement or reward educational effort
  • • Lack of recognition for the time and skill invested in creating resources

Our Solution

Teacher Notes Zambia provides a centralized, moderated library of PDF-based learning materials aligned with the Zambian ECZ curriculum. We've built a platform that ensures quality, accessibility, and fairness for all stakeholders.

How It Works

  1. 1. Teachers upload structured PDF notes organized by subject and grade
  2. 2. Admins verify and approve content for quality and curriculum alignment
  3. 3. Students access materials in a clean, distraction-free environment
  4. 4. Teacher engagement is tracked transparently and fairly
  5. 5. Earnings are calculated and distributed based on genuine student engagement

Key Features

  • ✓ ECZ Curriculum Aligned: All content matches official syllabus requirements
  • ✓ Quality Assured: Admin review ensures accuracy and relevance
  • ✓ Mobile Optimized: Access from any device with responsive design
  • ✓ Transparent Analytics: Teachers can track their impact and earnings
  • ✓ Secure & Fair: Fraud-resistant systems protect both creators and learners

How Teacher Monetization Works

Teachers earn money based on qualified views, not downloads. This ensures fairness and prevents abuse while rewarding genuine educational impact.

What Counts as a Qualified View?

  • ✓ A student must be logged into their verified account
  • ✓ Each student can only generate one paid view per document (prevents gaming the system)
  • ✓ Views are counted when a document is opened and recorded by the system
  • ✓ Earnings are calculated and aggregated daily for transparency

Example Calculation

If a document receives 10 qualified views and the platform rate is ZMW 10 per 10 views:

Gross Earnings: ZMW 10.00

Platform Commission (e.g., 20%): ZMW 2.00

Teacher Net Earnings: ZMW 8.00

Note: Commission rates and view thresholds may vary. Teachers can view current rates in their dashboard.

Why This Model Works

Fair to Teachers

Popular, helpful content earns more, rewarding quality over quantity

Sustainable

Platform can maintain quality control and technological infrastructure

Fraud-Resistant

One view per student prevents artificial inflation of earnings

Platform Roles

📚

Students

  • • Browse learning materials by subject, grade, and topic
  • • Read and study PDFs online
  • • Access exam preparation resources
  • • Free account creation
  • • No upload permissions
  • • No earnings (consumers of content)
👨‍🏫

Teachers

  • • Upload curriculum-aligned PDF notes
  • • Track views and earnings in real-time
  • • Request payouts when threshold is met
  • • Optional professional verification
  • • View detailed analytics on content performance
  • • Earn from quality, engaging content
⚙️

Administrators

  • • Review and approve/reject uploaded content
  • • Manage monetization rates and thresholds
  • • Process teacher payout requests
  • • Monitor platform for abuse and fraud
  • • Maintain content quality standards
  • • Ensure curriculum alignment

Technology & Architecture

Core Technologies

  • ▸ Tailwind CSS: Mobile-first responsive UI framework

Key Features

  • ▸ Daily Earnings Aggregation: Automated calculation and tracking
  • ▸ Fraud-Resistant View Tracking: Prevents manipulation and abuse

  • ▸ Analytics Dashboard: Real-time insights for teachers
