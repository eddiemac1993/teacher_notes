About Teacher Notes Zambia

Teacher Notes Zambia is a digital learning platform that improves access to exam-ready, curriculum-aligned PDF learning materials while creating a fair, transparent way for teachers to earn from their professional work.

Browse Materials Join as a Teacher

Note: “ECZ aligned” refers to alignment with Zambia’s curriculum and exam format. Teacher Notes Zambia is an independent platform.

Vision

A Zambia where every learner can access affordable, verified, exam-ready learning resources—and where teachers are recognized and rewarded for producing high-quality educational content.

Mission

Build a sustainable ecosystem that connects dedicated teachers with learners through a moderated library of structured PDFs, strong quality controls, and transparent engagement-based monetization.

The problem we solve

For students

  • Hard to find reliable notes aligned to syllabus outcomes and exam format.
  • Limited access to quality revision materials—especially outside major towns.
  • Cost barriers: textbooks and supplementary materials can be expensive.
  • Uncertainty about content quality and exam relevance.

For teachers

  • Materials are scattered across WhatsApp, USBs, and personal drives—no central library.
  • Teachers share resources but don’t get compensated for time and expertise.
  • No trusted mechanism to measure engagement fairly and prevent fraud.
  • Limited recognition for producing high-impact resources.

Our solution

Teacher Notes Zambia provides a centralized, moderated library of PDF learning materials organized by grade, subject, and topic. We focus on three outcomes: quality, access, and fair rewards.

How it works

  1. 1 Teachers upload structured PDFs (subject, grade, topic, and title).
  2. 2 Admins review and approve content for quality and alignment.
  3. 3 Students browse and read approved materials.
  4. 4 Qualified engagement is tracked and aggregated transparently.
  5. 5 Teachers request payouts once they meet the minimum threshold.

Key principles

  • Curriculum-first: organized by grade/subject/topic for exam readiness.
  • Quality controls: admin review to protect students and teachers.
  • Fair analytics: teachers see views and earnings clearly.
  • Fraud resistance: controlled counting rules reduce manipulation.
  • Mobile-first UX: fast, simple, distraction-free reading experience.

How teacher monetization works

Teachers earn from qualified views—not raw downloads. This protects the system and rewards real learning engagement. Rates, commission, and minimum payout are configurable by the platform administrators and are visible in the teacher dashboard.

What counts

  • User must be logged in.
  • Teacher’s own views do not count.
  • One qualified view per user per document (prevents repeat abuse).

Why it’s fair

  • Rewards quality content that learners actually use.
  • Transparent daily aggregation for accountability.
  • Scales sustainably as the library grows.

Presentation tip: Avoid hard-coded example amounts that could conflict with your admin settings. If you want an example, keep it general (“Earnings are based on qualified views and current platform rate per 1,000 views.”).

Roles on the platform

Students
Browse and read approved materials. No uploads. No earnings.
Teachers
Upload PDFs, see analytics, earn from qualified engagement, request payouts.
Administrators
Approve content, manage monetization, handle payouts, monitor abuse.
