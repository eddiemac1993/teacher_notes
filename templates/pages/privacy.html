Privacy Policy

Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

Teacher Notes Zambia (“we”, “our”, “the platform”) provides access to PDF learning materials uploaded by teachers. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account info: username, email, role (student/teacher), verification status (teachers).
  • Content: PDFs uploaded by teachers, titles, subjects, grade levels, topics, optional external links.
  • Usage data: view sessions for fraud prevention and monetization (e.g., timestamp, interaction flags, hashed IP/user-agent).

2. How we use your information

  • To provide and improve the service (login, browsing, upload, moderation).
  • To calculate teacher earnings from counted/qualified views.
  • To prevent abuse (duplicate view attempts, automated/bot traffic).
  • To respond to support requests.

3. View tracking & monetization

We record view sessions to ensure fair monetization. A counted view may be limited (e.g., one per user per post). Anti-fraud signals like IP/user-agent may be stored in hashed form.

4. Data retention

We keep essential records for platform integrity, earnings audits, and legal compliance. You may request account deletion where permitted; certain records (e.g., payout logs) may be retained for audit.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited information with payment processors to complete payouts, or where required by law.

6. Security

We apply reasonable technical measures to protect stored data. However, no system is 100% secure.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact support using the Support page.

This document is a platform policy draft. For production, review with legal counsel (especially for Zambia data protection requirements).
