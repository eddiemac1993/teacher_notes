Teacher Guidelines

How to upload quality content and earn fairly.

1) Allowed Content

  • Only PDF documents (max 5 MB per file).
  • One PDF per post.
  • Clear title, subject, grade level, and topic.
  • Optional: external video link (the platform does not host videos).

2) Quality Requirements

  • Readable scans (avoid blurry images).
  • Correct subject/grade selection.
  • No duplicated uploads of the same file.
  • Use a descriptive topic (e.g., “Fractions: Adding & Subtracting”).

3) Copyright & Ownership

Upload only materials you created or have permission to share. Do not upload copyrighted textbooks or paid notes you don’t own. We may remove content and suspend accounts for violations.

4) Monetization Rules

  • Teacher views do not count.
  • Duplicate views by the same student may not count.
  • Suspicious traffic may be filtered.
  • Earnings are calculated from counted/qualified views based on platform settings.

5) Verification

Some features (like payout requests) may require teacher verification. Maintain accurate profile info and respond to admin requests.

Tip: upload high-value exam-focused topics; these get more student views and higher earnings.
